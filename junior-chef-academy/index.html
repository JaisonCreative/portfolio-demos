🌟 Timer Tips

Set the 5-min timer while your butter melts or water comes to a boil. Use 10 min for chopping and prepping ingredients before you start cooking. The 15-min timer works great for simmering soups or baking cookies. Pick 25 min when you're cooking a full meal from start to finish — start the timer, follow your recipe steps, and you'll be done before it rings!
